Privacy

Last updated October 15, 2026

We keep this simple, and we treat your information with care.

What we collect

When you buy a series, our payment processor (Stripe) collects your name, email, and payment details. We receive your name and email so we can send your emails. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

We use your email to deliver the series you purchased and, occasionally, to tell you about new series. You can unsubscribe from any email with one click.

Who we share it with

Only the services that make this work: Stripe for payments and Kit for sending email. We never sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by writing to hello@alamptomyfeet.com.
