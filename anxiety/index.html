Questions

Good to know

When will the emails arrive?

Your first email comes the morning after you purchase, at 6 AM (Arizona time), and one arrives every morning after that for 30 days.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a single $9 purchase for the full 30 days. Nothing renews.

Which Bible translation do you use?

Every passage is from the English Standard Version (ESV).

Do I need any Bible background?

Not at all. Each email explains what you need to know, so it works whether you've read the Bible for decades or are just starting.

Is this a replacement for counseling or medical care?

No. These emails are for meeting God in His word. If anxiety is heavy or persistent, please also reach out to a pastor, counselor, or doctor. Seeking help is not a lack of faith.

What if the emails don't show up?

Check your promotions or spam folder first, and add hello@alamptomyfeet.com to your contacts. If you still can't find them, write to us and we'll sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If it isn't what you hoped, email us within 3 days and we'll refund you, no questions asked.
